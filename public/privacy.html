AI RoboPet Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what AI RoboPet ("the app"), published by Psychemulation Enterprises, collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the web app and the Android app on Google Play.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google or email, we store your name, email address, a screen name and a user ID from Firebase Authentication.
  • Your builds. The robot type, goal, budget and parts lists you save are stored in your profile. If you are signed out, they are kept only in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • Content you publish. Projects, posts, photos, likes and comments you share to the community are visible to other users.
  • AI requests. The text and settings you send to AI features (sourcing, compatibility checks, the LLM brain, code, image and video generation) are sent to our server and on to Google's Gemini API to produce a response.
  • Reports. If you report content, we store the report, the reason and your user ID if you are signed in.
  • Google Drive and Tasks. Only if you choose to export or sync, the app accesses the files or task lists it creates for you, using permissions you grant at that moment.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or run third-party analytics or ad trackers.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database) — Firebase privacy
  • Google Gemini API (AI features) — Gemini API terms
  • PayPal, only if you choose to support the creator through the external PayPal link.

How long we keep it

Account and build data is kept until you delete your account. Reports are kept as long as needed to review them and protect the community.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open your profile, choose Delete account, and confirm. This removes your profile, saved builds and the projects you published. Comments you left on other people's projects stay visible under your screen name. See how to delete your account for other ways to ask.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored in Google Cloud. Database rules restrict each profile to its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
